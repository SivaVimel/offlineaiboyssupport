Support

RentBestie is an offline AI companion app. Everything below is answered honestly, including the things the app is not good at.

Can't find your answer? Email sivavimelrajhen@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version.

Getting started. Scroll the Home feed and tap Follow on anyone you like — or double-tap his photo, or tap the + on an avatar in the top row. Tap a post to read his profile. Everyone you follow appears under Following in the tab bar, where you can say hi.

Using the app

How do I start a conversation?

Open Following in the tab bar and tap Say hi next to his name. You can also tap Send … a message under any post, or open a profile and tap Message. Messaging someone follows him automatically.

Where did someone I followed go from my feed?

People you follow move out of the Home feed the next time it loads — when you reopen the app or pull down to refresh — because they are waiting for you in Following. They never disappear the moment you tap Follow. Unfollow someone (the ••• button in Following, or the Following button on his profile) and he returns to the feed.

Why does the feed open partway down?

The feed remembers where you were and reopens there, so you carry on with new faces instead of starting from the top. Scroll up to go back. Settings → Erase everything also resets it to the top.

How do I find a particular person?

Open Explore and search by name, home town, job or interest.

I marked someone Not interested by mistake

Go to Settings → Not interested. Everyone you hid is listed there with a Bring back button. Nothing is ever deleted. There is also Bring back everyone I passed on if you want them all at once.

The feed says I'm all caught up

That means everyone is either in Following or marked Not interested. Chat from Following, or bring people back from Settings.

How do I open his profile from a chat?

Tap his name at the top of the conversation, or tap his picture next to any of his messages.

The AI

Why does the first launch take a moment?

The language model is loaded into memory when the app opens. While that happens a chat shows Connecting instead of the message box. It is usually a few seconds, and longer the first time after installing.

Why do replies take a few seconds?

They are being written on your device rather than fetched from a server. That is slower than a cloud service, particularly on older hardware, and it is why the app works with no signal. You can tap the stop button at any time.

He repeated himself, or got something wrong

The model is small enough to run on a phone, and small models make things up, lose the thread and contradict themselves. He remembers the last few messages in a conversation, not the whole history. This is a limitation of running privately on your device, not a fault in your copy of the app.

Are these real people?

No. Every companion is fictional. The photographs are AI-generated and depict no real person, and the names, home towns and life stories are invented. RentBestie is a companionship app, not a service for meeting real people.

Privacy and data

Does anything I type leave my device?

No. The app contains no networking code, so it cannot send your messages anywhere. It works fully in airplane mode.

Will the character photos show up in my Photos app?

No. They live inside the app and are never written to your photo library. The app has no photo library access at all.

How do I delete my conversations?

Settings → Delete all conversations clears every chat and keeps your Following list. Settings → Erase everything clears chats, follows, your Not interested list and your place in the feed together. Deleting the app removes everything.

Troubleshooting

The app is large

Around 1.6 GB. The language model and every character photograph ship inside the app so it works the first time you open it, with no download and no account. Install over Wi-Fi.

It says it couldn't reach him

The model failed to load. Tap Retry in the message bar. If it keeps happening, restart your device, and if it still fails, delete and reinstall the app — your conversations are stored on the device and will not survive a reinstall, so this is a last resort.

The app feels slow or warm

Generating text uses the processor heavily. Close other apps, and expect older devices to be noticeably slower. The app does no background work — it stops when you leave it.
